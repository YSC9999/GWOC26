# **Business Overview**

**Business Name:** Basho by Shivangi  
**Industry / Domain:** Handcrafted Pottery, Japanese-Inspired Tableware, Art & Culture  
**Instagram Handle:** https://www.instagram.com/bashobyyshivangi/

Basho is a pottery and tableware brand inspired by Japanese culture and the philosophy of the Japanese poet *Matsuo Bashō*. The brand focuses on handcrafted, raw, earthy ceramic pieces including custom and ready-made tableware, workshops, studio experiences, and curated cultural events.

**Primary Goal of the Website**

1. Brand Awareness  
2. Product Sales (Tableware & Custom Orders)  
3. Workshop Registrations  
4. Studio & Experience Bookings  
5. Corporate & Event Collaborations

**Core Website Features & Pages**

**A. Informational Features**

* **Home Page:** Introduction to Basho’s philosophy, Japanese inspiration, handcrafted pottery, and brand essence.  
    
* *About the Brand & Founder:*  
1. *Story of Basho*  
2. *Inspiration from Japanese poetry and culture*  
3. *Founder’s journey and vision*  
     
* *Japanese Culture & Philosophy: Content explaining Japanese aesthetics, minimalism, clay theory, and craftsmanship values reflected in Basho products.*  
    
* *Products Overview:*  
  *Introduction to tableware collections and custom-made pottery.*  
    
* *Artisanship & After-Care:*  
1. *Craft process*  
2. *Materials used*  
3. *Food-safe, microwave-safe, dishwasher-safe details*  
4. *Product care and longevity*

**Product & Commerce Features**

**Product Listing Pages:**

* Ready-made tableware  
* Custom-made pottery  
* Detailed product descriptions  
* Material, usage, and care information

**Custom Order System:**

* Form to take custom requirements  
* Reference images / notes  
* Previous custom order showcase

**Pricing & GST:**

* Product pricing  
* GST number taking as optional field to give out invoice  
* Shipping cost calculation based on weight (per kg).

**Payment Integration:**

* Razorpay payment gateway

**Order Communication:**

* Automated email to sender and receiver  
* Order confirmation and status updates

**Backend / Admin Panel Requirements**

* Product management (add/edit/remove products)  
* Custom order request management  
* Workshop registration management  
* Payment & order tracking  
* Customer database management  
* Shipping & GST handling  
* Event & exhibition updates add and remove  
* Gallery and testimonial management

**Design Layout**

1. Raw, earthy, artisanal, and culture-driven experience  
2. Scroll-based storytelling that reflects craftsmanship and process  
3. Strong focus on visuals, textures, and product photography  
4. Subtle animations to enhance experience without overpowering art  
5. Clean flow guiding users from brand story → products → experiences → purchase/registration

## **Mandatory Constraints for Students**

1. Website must support both **e-commerce and experience bookings**  
2. Razorpay integration is mandatory  
   Custom order and workshop registration forms required  
3. Visual storytelling is as important as functionality  
4. Performance and mobile responsiveness are essential
